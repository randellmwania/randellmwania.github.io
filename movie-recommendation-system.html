The Problem

Modern digital media catalogs face the "infinite scroll paradox"—users become overwhelmed by thousands of choices, leading to abandonment and lower session retention. Simple top-10 trending lists fail because individual taste is inherently multi-faceted and nuanced.

Hybrid Algorithmic Approach

To build an engine that balances serendipitous discovery with deep thematic consistency, this project implemented a hybrid approach:

  • User-Item Collaborative Filtering: Analyzed implicit and explicit rating matrices to identify affinity clusters among users with matching taste fingerprints.
  • Content-Based Vectorization: Processed film metadata, narrative keywords, cast ensembles, and genres into high-dimensional vector spaces using TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity.
  • Cold-Start Mitigation: When new users join without rating histories, the content-based module serves relevant recommendations based on initial preference selections before collaborative signals mature.

Key Outcomes

Validation testing demonstrated a 30% increase in recommendation accuracy over standard matrix factorization baselines, significantly improving hit rate and diversity metrics across long-tail catalog titles.
