The Operational Challenge

At Robert Bosch East Africa, supporting three distinct corporate divisions (Mobility, Power Tools, Home Comfort) alongside sister entity BSH South Africa required seamless IT and facility logistics. Crucial processes like laptop allocations, cost recharging, and equipment repairs suffered from disjointed communication channels, manual data entry errors, and delayed escalations.

Low-Code Engineering & ITSM

To streamline service delivery without incurring prohibitive SaaS licensing overhead, the initiative leveraged Microsoft Power Platform and enterprise ITIL methodologies:

  • Custom Power Apps Development: Built bespoke front-end portals for Facility Management (FCM) and BSH South Africa to submit asset requisitions and track audit histories.
  • Automated Workflows: Orchestrated automated approval matrices in Power Automate, instantly notifying department heads, generating sign-off documentation, and updating CMDB hardware states.
  • CMDB & IT Financial Management (ITFM): Established rigorous automated ledger logs ensuring hardware assets (laptops, mobile units, specialized peripherals) are accurately cost-recharged to the appropriate business unit.
  • SMT/ITSP Tier 1-3 Support: Maintained high first-contact resolution rates and clear escalation paths for complex infrastructure incidents.

Business Impact

The digital transformation achieved 100% audit compliance for deployed IT assets, reduced asset turnaround times by over 40%, and eliminated 10+ hours of repetitive weekly manual tracking.
